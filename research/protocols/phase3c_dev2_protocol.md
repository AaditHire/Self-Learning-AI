# Phase 3C-DEV2 — primitive-matched composition generalization study

**Status: frozen proposal for independent review; no model execution authorized.** This is an A-only exploratory development study. The Phase 3C confirmatory A gate remains a STOP; Phase 3C `EVAL_A` and Phase 3C-DEV1 development are consumed. This design does not start B, replay, retention, or any other continual-learning intervention. The sealed final-paper holdout remains unopened.

## Scientific question and hypotheses

When primitive and GOCO API exposure are held equal, does explicit practice combining predicates improve transfer to unseen combinations compared with mostly separate/simple predicate use?

The primary descriptive estimand is, for each fixed paired seed, **COMPOSITION minus ISOLATED pass@1** over the 16 fresh `NOVEL_COMPOSITION` development IDs, then the unweighted mean of the three seed differences in percentage points. Directional development hypothesis H1: the difference is positive. H0: no improvement or a negative difference. Neither hypothesis defines a paper-level PASS gate, significance claim, or permission to resume continual learning. A positive contrast with poor primitive sanity, strong nearest-neighbor similarity, or only one archetype gaining will be interpreted narrowly.

Prompt wording and target semantics necessarily differ with that contrast and are residual confounds to report.

## Fresh construction and exact conditions

[build_phase3c_dev2_data.py](../../scripts/build_phase3c_dev2_data.py) deterministically emits the committed JSON. It derives new programs from a four-predicate design per subskill, with independent IDs, prompts, references and five semantic cases. It does not import or minimally rewrite DEV1 evaluation programs. The numeric predicates over indices `1..n` are: odd index `i%2==1`; remainder two modulo three `i%3==2`; divisor of input `n%i==0`; first-half index `2*i<=n`. The array predicates over four parsed values are: negative `values[i]<0`; even value `values[i]%2==0`; squared magnitude above four `values[i]*values[i]>4`; value greater than its zero-based position `values[i]>i`. Each pair among the four predicates is used once per subskill, yielding six archetypes × five constant variants = 30 examples. Pair IDs 1, 3 and 5 traverse forward; 2, 4 and 6 traverse backward, identically in both conditions. The array programs use `IMPORT strings`, `strings.SPLIT`, `strings.TO_NUMBER`, indexing and a four-element array. No `math` import or namespaced math call is needed; multiplication and modulo are GOCO expressions. The five array inputs used to validate development references differ from the five used for training references.

For an ordered pair `(P,Q)` and offset `k=1..5`, ISOLATED computes `count(P)+count(Q)+k`. The reference has a redundant `P` guard nested inside `P` and an independent `Q` guard. The redundancy is deliberate: it exposes the same nested-IF form, predicate spellings and literal occurrence frequencies as COMPOSITION while keeping the two requested counts independent. COMPOSITION computes `count(P AND Q)+count(P)+k`, using nested `P→Q` guards plus a separate `P` count. Both references have two accumulators, one loop, three `IF` tokens, one nested IF, two textual occurrences of P and one of Q, and the same I/O/API scaffold. This is a controlled but artificial isolated baseline; the redundant guard may itself influence learning and is an explicit limitation.

| Pair archetype | Predicates | Loop form | Variants per condition/subskill |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `pair1` | 1 + 2 | forward | 5 |
| `pair2` | 1 + 3 | reverse | 5 |
| `pair3` | 1 + 4 | forward | 5 |
| `pair4` | 2 + 3 | reverse | 5 |
| `pair5` | 2 + 4 | forward | 5 |
| `pair6` | 3 + 4 | reverse | 5 |

The [pre-gradient audit](../results/PHASE_3C_DEV2/pre_gradient_audit.json) gives each primitive's exact containing-example count, textual reference occurrence count, archetypes and API association. Every one of eight predicates appears in **15 examples in each condition**. Within each subskill, predicates 1–4 occur **30, 25, 20, 15** times respectively in each condition; this unequal *within-condition* distribution follows ordered pair placement, and the **between-condition** frequency is exact for every predicate. The audit also reports exactly matched imports, namespaced functions, indexing, IFs, loops, reverse loops and nested IFs. Prospective tolerances before gradients are **zero** missing primitives, **zero** differences in containing-example counts, textual primitive occurrences, and audited GOCO construct counts, exactly 30 examples per subskill, and exactly 60 examples per condition. If a file or recount violates these bounds, STOP and return for redesign. This matching controls availability, not semantic equivalence or token-level optimization difficulty.

## Fresh development suite

The 48 committed development IDs are balanced: 24 numeric and 24 array; 16 IDs in each prospectively assigned group (eight per subskill). Constants vary within an archetype and are **not** treated as independent structures. The suite is frozen before any model use and becomes `DEVELOPMENT_CONSUMED` upon execution.

| Group | Per subskill | Purpose and exact construction |
|---|---:|---|
| `PRIMITIVE_SANITY` | four single-predicate archetypes × two variants = 8 | One count per predicate; checks basic primitive/API use, not a paper-level acquisition gate. |
| `NOVEL_COMPOSITION` | two archetypes × four variants = 8 | Count two distinct predicate intersections in one program. The two four-predicate pairings are `(1&2)+(3&4)` and `(1&3)+(2&4)`. Each component predicate occurs in both training conditions; neither complete four-predicate composition occurs in either. One archetype traverses forward, the other reverse. |
| `STRUCTURAL_TRANSFER` | two archetypes × four variants = 8 | One reverses traversal for the `(1&2)` intersection relative to its training pair; the other computes separate `(1)+(3)` counts in two passes. Both use represented predicates and familiar GOCO constructs under a changed arrangement. |

The central 32 `NOVEL_COMPOSITION` and `STRUCTURAL_TRANSFER` IDs have an explicit per-task machine-readable [coverage proof](../results/PHASE_3C_DEV2/pre_gradient_audit.json): every required predicate is represented in **both** conditions. The 16 novel-composition IDs have no exact full composition signature in either training set. This was established from the reference algorithms before model execution; no outcome was used to relabel a group. Structural-transfer IDs may contain a pair relationship seen in COMPOSITION by design; their question is transfer across control/data arrangement, not wholly unseen predicates.

## Descriptive outcomes and statistical plan

After separate authorization, evaluate each final adapter exactly once with greedy pass@1 on its own 60 training IDs and the common 48-ID development suite, using the frozen prompt, no repair/retry, the pinned compiler and five cases per task. Save raw generation, compiler stdout/stderr, case outcomes, task ID, seed, condition, exact target reproduction on own training, and failure stage (parse/syntax, compiler-semantic, execution/runtime, hidden-semantic, success). PASS for an ID requires compilation, execution and all five cases. No single aggregate score substitutes for subskill, group or archetype reporting.

Report for every seed: own-training pass@1 and exact target reproduction, Primitive Sanity, Novel Composition, Structural Transfer, overall 48-ID development pass@1, numeric and array separately, all 16 development archetypes, and the full failure-stage distribution. Report the primary Novel Composition COMPOSITION−ISOLATED paired percentage-point effect and each seed difference. The mean across three prespecified seeds is descriptive. Preserve paired task IDs and both conditions in all comparisons. For uncertainty, run **10,000 task-cluster bootstrap resamples with seed 20281221**, stratified by group×subskill; each sampled task ID carries all six seed×condition outcomes. For the primary effect, resample within the four Novel Composition archetypes (four task IDs each), preserving archetype balance. Report percentile 2.5/97.5 intervals. As a structural-correlation sensitivity, resample whole archetype blocks within subskill, preserving all variants and seed/condition outcomes; four primary blocks make this unstable and it must be labeled so. Show each archetype's raw rates. No seed-task-pair independence assumption, hypothesis-test p-value, winner selection gate, or paper-level PASS threshold is allowed. A missing outcome or incomplete seed-condition cell is reported as deviation, not imputed or rerun silently.

Interpretation is bounded prospectively: a gain with comparable primitive sanity and multiple novel-composition archetypes supports further development of composition practice; equal low novel scores mean this recipe did not solve transfer; poor COMPOSITION own-training fit suggests insufficient acquisition under the fixed recipe; a primitive-sanity gap weakens matching interpretation; gain concentrated in highly similar neighbors remains local; syntax-to-semantic shifts do not identify internal mechanism. Report all patterns regardless of direction. No result from DEV2 repairs Phase 3C's confirmatory STOP.

## STOP logic, firewall and limitations

**STOP before gradients** on a failed reference/case, exact forbidden reuse, central missing primitive, unequal primitive/API frequencies outside the exact tolerances above, any altered frozen input hash, schedule/count/seed imbalance, changed compiler/base/tokenizer/prompt, overlength sequence, or >10% token-exposure difference. Do not repair using model outcomes. **STOP now** for independent design review; no training or scientific model evaluation is part of this commit.

The main limitations are a hand-built four-predicate-per-subskill domain, artificial repeated-guard ISOLATED examples, 16 central IDs but only four central structures, constant-variant clustering, different prompt semantics, a 24-step budget, five-case references, three fixed seeds, and possible local code similarity despite no exact reuse. The comparator tests one specific way to present separate counts against one specific nested-intersection practice; it does not identify a general theory of composition. Structural-transfer tasks reuse known constructs in new arrangements, but may differ in difficulty from novel-composition tasks. No neural mechanism, broad GOCO competence, continual-learning retention, or replay effect can be inferred.

After any separately authorized development execution, mark the DEV2 suite consumed. A future confirmatory retention experiment would require a selected and frozen A recipe, a **new untouched** acquisition/retention benchmark and independent review before gradients. This design does not construct that benchmark.
