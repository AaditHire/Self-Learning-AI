## 3. PREFIX — required scientific topology

`prefix_running_pair(P,Q)` requires **exactly ONE logical ordered traversal phase** over the complete domain in §2. Before traversal, initialize two distinct semantic states to zero: the prior-P count and the result accumulator. They cannot be merged, substituted by a Boolean existence flag, or treated as two names for one state.

For each ordered item, the scientific topology must retain all nine distinct obligations:

1. The prior-P count carried from the preceding item is available as `prior_p_before_i`.
2. The result accumulator carried from the preceding item is separately available as `result_before_i`.
3. The current item's `P_i` truth is evaluated under the frozen P predicate.
4. The current item's `Q_i` truth is evaluated under the frozen Q predicate.
5. The Q-dependent contribution consumes **PRE-UPDATE** prior-P state: `contribution_i = Q_i * prior_p_before_i`.
6. That contribution updates the separate result state: `result_after_i = result_before_i + contribution_i`.
7. Subsequently, the current P truth updates prior-P state: `prior_p_after_i = prior_p_before_i + P_i`.
8. Both updated states carry to the next ordered item, without resetting or replacing either recurrence.
9. The final result is emitted only after the complete traversal finishes.

The required temporal/data relation is:

```text
prior_p_before_i → Q-dependent contribution → result update
prior_p_before_i + P_i → prior_p_after_i, subsequently
result_after_i and prior_p_after_i → next ordered iteration
completed result → final output
```

The Q contribution never consumes `prior_p_after_i`. Same-item P therefore cannot flow into its own Q contribution, even when both truths are true. The result update precedes the P-state update in the required logical topology. Pure P/Q truth evaluation may retain the companion proposal's qualified ordering freedom, provided the strict read/update relation, both state identities, source obligations and all frozen requirements remain intact. That freedom does not reorder state updates or admit a different traversal.

## 4. TWO_PASS — required scientific topology

`two_pass_product(P,Q)` requires **exactly TWO LOGICAL COMPLETE TRAVERSAL PHASES**, in the order below. This is an explicit **NEW prospective structural specification**, not a rule recovered from the label `two_pass_product` or historical implementation.

### 4.1 Phase 1: complete P aggregation

Initialize `countP = 0` before Phase 1's first item. Traverse the entire domain in §2 in its required order. Evaluate P for each item and add its 0/1 truth to the P-count state, carrying that state between consecutive items. P-count is the only predicate-count aggregation state updated in this phase. Ordinary domain/index control is retained; “only” does not erase decoding or iteration control. No Q aggregation, result accumulation or partial-count multiplication is performed in Phase 1. Finish with the completed final P count.

### 4.2 Phase boundary: completed P persists

Phase 1 completes **before Phase 2 begins**. Its completed P count must exist at this boundary and persist unchanged through Phase 2 into final composition. P-count is no longer updated after Phase 1. The phase boundary, count completion and persistence are scientific facts; they cannot be erased by treating the counts as two abstract cardinalities. The boundary is not a newly invented atomic macro or EDGE label.

### 4.3 Phase 2: complete Q aggregation

At Phase 2 entry, initialize the distinct Q-count state to `countQ = 0` before its first item. Traverse the same entire domain again, in the same order. Evaluate Q for each item and update Q-count with its 0/1 truth, carrying Q-count between consecutive items. No P-count update or result multiplication occurs in this phase. Finish with the completed final Q count. Scientific initialization, phase membership, state lifetime and completion must be represented explicitly; a source declaration by itself does not establish a valid phase-entry/state mapping.

### 4.4 Final composition and output

Only after **both** phases complete, calculate `result = countP * countQ`. Its operands are the completed Phase-1 P count and completed Phase-2 Q count. Emit the result only after that multiplication. The companion law's commutativity of completed multiplication operands remains mathematical symmetry subject to frozen V3.3; it does **not** permit Q-first then P-second traversal.

### 4.5 Excluded scientific alternatives

The following do **not** instantiate TWO_PASS even where they return the same integer: one loop maintaining P and Q counters simultaneously; interleaved aggregation phases; Q-first then P-second; multiplication of evolving partial counts; accumulating per-item `P_i * Q_i`; recomputing a completed count inside the other pass; loop fusion; and an algebraic product of abstract cardinalities that omits the two required traversal/state phases. Required domain coverage, phase identity, carry, completion and final consumption cannot be replaced by output agreement. The source/compiler boundary in §3.1 applies here as well, without authorizing loop fusion in the scientific target or its evidence.
