# Phase 2B frozen multi-seed confirmatory experiment

Status at freeze: preregistered; no Phase 2B gradient step or model inference has occurred.

## Question and scope

Does a fixed QLoRA procedure trained on fresh, verified GOCO examples reproducibly improve no-documentation hidden-test pass@1 on a new structurally held-out benchmark relative to the immutable parent model?

This is the first confirmatory parameter-acquisition experiment. It does not revise the failed Phase 1T gate and cannot establish continual learning, autonomous learning, catastrophic-forgetting prevention, or general lifelong learning. Sequential learning and continual-learning methods are prohibited.

## Separation gate

Splits are generated by distinct semantic families, algorithm labels, generator lineages, structural signatures, AST proxies, control-flow labels, semantic-operation combinations, prompts, and references; they are not random partitions. The final audit has zero exact overlap in prompts, algorithm labels, lineages, structural signatures, or semantic-operation labels. No normalized-code train/evaluation or prior-suite pair reaches 0.98. All lower-threshold flags were manually reviewed before freeze.

Each confirmatory task is assigned a frozen normalized-code distance bucket: far below 0.70, medium from 0.70 through below 0.85, and near at or above 0.85. Criterion 5 passes only if there is no rejected/undisclosed duplicate and either at least half of all successful seed-task instances are medium/far or mean improvement on medium/far tasks is at least 10 percentage points.

## Training

Three independent adapters use the identical data, order-generation procedure, immutable base, and Phase 2A recipe. Only the random seed changes: `20260921`, `20261007`, and `20261103`.

The fixed recipe is NF4 4-bit with double quantization and FP16 compute; LoRA rank 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05, bias none, and targets `q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, `o_proj`, `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, `down_proj`; three epochs; micro-batch one; gradient accumulation eight; maximum length 320; gradient checkpointing; PagedAdamW8bit; learning rate 0.0002; zero weight decay; betas 0.9/0.999; epsilon 1e-8; max gradient norm 1.0; linear schedule; five warmup steps. The final epoch is selected a priori. There is no search, early stopping, development evaluation, seed selection, or adapter merge.

Each run must complete 75 optimizer steps with finite logged losses and gradients. Failure is retained and counts against the all-three-runs feasibility condition; no replacement seed is permitted under this protocol.

## Conditions

The 128 tasks run once each with greedy pass@1 under:

1. immutable base, no GOCO documentation or retrieval;
2. seed `20260921` adapter, no documentation or retrieval;
3. seed `20261007` adapter, no documentation or retrieval;
4. seed `20261103` adapter, no documentation or retrieval; and
5. immutable base plus the exact frozen Candidate C documentation, descriptive only.

There is no retry or manual repair. Base and all adapters use the same prompt template, normalization, compiler, tests, timeout, and token limits. Condition 5 cannot affect selection or the success decision.

## Metrics and analysis

Primary outcome is task-level hidden-test pass@1. Parse, compile, execution, family, paired base/adapted outcome, and failure class are secondary. Failure classes are wrong language, lexical, syntax, semantic validation, runtime, timeout/output limit/system, structural requirement, hidden-test semantic failure, and success.

Every seed is reported separately. The arithmetic mean and range across seeds are reported overall and by family. Statistical uncertainty uses a paired task-cluster bootstrap: tasks are resampled, and the three seeds remain repeated models inside each sampled task. Seeds are not treated as 384 independent observations.

Training pass@1, confirmatory pass@1, and their absolute gap are reported per seed. Regression accuracy and every change from the base are reported per seed. A regression drop strictly greater than 15 percentage points is severe.

## Conjunctive confirmation rule

The acquisition effect is confirmed only if all conditions hold:

1. mean adapted no-doc pass@1 is at least 20%;
2. mean absolute improvement over base/no-doc is at least 15 percentage points;
3. every seed improves by at least 10 percentage points;
4. every seed passes at least one task in at least four families;
5. the result passes the frozen structural-distance rule above; and
6. no seed has a non-GOCO regression drop greater than 15 percentage points.

All three training runs must also be feasible. Thresholds cannot change after results are observed. A failed or incomplete condition yields confirmatory FAIL.
