# Phase 3C proposed preregistration: retention-valid fixed-budget replay replication

**Status:** proposed and frozen for independent review before Phase 3C gradients or model evaluation. Execution requires independent approval of this exact protocol and its hashes. The Phase 3B completed result and infrastructure checkpoint are historical inputs, not Phase 3C observations. The final-paper holdout remains sealed.

## Question, rationale, hypotheses

The question is whether fixed 20% A replay during B training preserves **the exact A task IDs passed immediately before B** better than a compute-matched naive B branch, after A has been meaningfully acquired in both subskills, while allowing B learning. Phase 3B's aggregate A gain could not answer this: pre-B A was 8/32 per seed, numeric iteration was 0/16, and all 47 replay post-B A passes were pre-B failures.

H1 (co-primary retention): replay exceeds naive by at least **20 percentage points** in the unweighted three-seed mean of per-seed `PASS→PASS / pre-B PASS`, and replay retains at least **50%** of acquired A task IDs in every seed. H2 (co-primary aggregate A): replay exceeds naive by at least **10 percentage points** in unweighted three-seed mean full EVAL_A pass@1. These are conjunctive; aggregate gain without H1 is reacquisition/new learning, not preservation. H3 (qualified B plasticity): if the naive branch first meets the broad B acquisition gate below, replay retains at least 75% of naive mean B gain and shows B gains in both B subskills. H4 (safety): no branch crosses the frozen severe non-GOCO collapse boundary. These are operational thresholds, not claims of population-level statistical significance.

## Immutable materials and construction

Use `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct` at revision `488639f1ff808d1d3d0ba301aef8c11461451ec5`, the exact two local base-weight hashes in `phase3c_config.json`, immutable base weights, adapter-only NF4 QLoRA, and deterministic GOCO compiler jar SHA-256 `42478b3500ff31df65f411e4072f578be5fede844020392a664eb89865b2a2fb`. `scripts/build_phase3c_data.py` deterministically constructs fresh A and B data. Each capability has 60 training examples (30 per subskill; 15 in each of two training archetypes per subskill) and 32 confirmatory evaluation tasks (16 per subskill; 8 in each of two evaluation archetypes per subskill). Every item has five semantic cases, including edge/boundary inputs. A subskills are numeric iteration and array reduction. B subskills are string transformation and field processing. Training and evaluation archetype names and structural tags are in the item manifests; evaluation uses structurally different operations/compositions, not a held-out parameter from a training template. Evaluation task IDs and five hidden cases are fixed before any Phase 3C model run. No Phase 3A/3B evaluation prompt or reference is reused as a Phase 3C evaluation item, and no exact train/evaluation prompt or reference reuse is permitted. The final-paper holdout is excluded from all construction and auditing.

Before freezing, `scripts/validate_phase3c_data.py` executes **every** reference program against **every** hidden semantic case with the pinned compiler and records case-level status. The audit compares Phase 3C train/evaluation prompts and normalized reference structures against one another and all explicitly consumed pre-3C full-synthesis materials. Exact prompt duplication or normalized-code similarity `>=0.98` against prior consumed references or opposite split is a **STOP** for construction. The audit also reports all prompt similarities `>=0.70`, train/evaluation code similarities `>=0.85`, prior-code similarities `>=0.90`, normalized AST-proxy collisions, and distance buckets for every evaluation item. The `0.98` rejection rule is not used to hide lower-threshold overlap. `research/results/EXP-0048/structural_overlap_manual_review.md` reviews those flags. This comparison cannot prove conceptual independence from all historical or unseen prompts; it documents a reproducible, bounded audit.

## Fixed training and evaluation sequence

Three preregistered seeds: **20260925, 20261013, 20261119**. For each seed, train one A adapter from the immutable base on the 60 fresh A training IDs, exactly three epochs, final checkpoint. The fixed A recipe is the Phase 3A/3B recipe: 4-bit NF4 with double quantization, float16 compute, rank-16 LoRA, alpha 32, dropout 0.05, no bias, seven projection modules (`q,k,v,o,gate,up,down`), micro batch 1, accumulation 8, sequence length 320, gradient checkpointing, PagedAdamW8bit, learning rate 0.0002, weight decay 0, betas (0.9,0.999), epsilon 1e-8, gradient norm 1, linear scheduler, five warmup optimizer steps. Three 60-slot epochs give eight steps per epoch and **24 steps** in each stage. This recipe is specified from prior development experience; no Phase 3C confirmatory score may tune it. No checkpoint selection on score, seed replacement, or retry with changed settings.

Evaluate each seed's post-A adapter **once** on frozen EVAL_A and EVAL_B before B. A task is PASS only if greedy single-shot output compiles and passes all five hidden semantic cases. Greedy decoding has `do_sample=false`, one beam, maximum input 8192 tokens, maximum new output 512 tokens, no retry, repair, documentation, or search. Same prompt, compiler, timeout (3 s), output cap (65536 bytes/stream), evaluator, and cases at every condition. Base A/B/regression measurements are descriptive controls, obtained once after execution is authorized; no base result can alter thresholds.

### Pre-B A acquisition eligibility: hard STOP

Every one of the three post-A checkpoints must satisfy all of:

1. At least **24/32** A tasks PASS overall (75%).
2. At least **10/16** PASS in **each** A subskill (62.5%).
3. At least **2/8** PASS in **each** of the four A evaluation archetypes.

The 24-task floor supplies at least 72 acquired seed-task instances for interpretable pass→pass analysis. The subskill and archetype floors prevent the one-archetype concentration and zero numeric acquisition observed in Phase 3B. The thresholds are chosen before Phase 3C model results and are demanding but below Phase 3A's prior overall A rates; those earlier rates are motivation, not borrowed Phase 3C data. If any seed fails any floor, publish that pre-B result, mark **STOP: acquisition ineligible**, and conduct **no B training**. Never change the A recipe, prompts, seed, data, threshold, or checkpoint after seeing Phase 3C scores.

### Matched B branches

For each eligible seed, fork **the exact same A adapter bytes** into naive B and 20%-A-replay B. Initialize optimizer and scheduler anew and identically for both branches, using the same stage-specific RNG policy and deterministic data loader. Freeze the parent A adapter SHA-256 and assert it matches on both forks. `phase3c_replay_schedule.json` fixes every per-seed, per-epoch example ID and slot before training. Each branch consumes 60 exposure slots per epoch, three epochs, 24 optimizer steps, micro batch 1 and accumulation 8. At each epoch, naive sees all 60 B IDs. Replay sees the same ordered B slots except **12 fixed replacements**: six B string and six B field slots become six A numeric and six A array IDs. Across three epochs, each seed replays 36 unique A examples; 48 B and 12 A slots per replay epoch. The only intended intervention difference is those slot replacements. No replay-ratio sweep, alternative intervention, or added compute.

Record exact slot order, stage/seed RNG state, step counts, optimizer and scheduler initialization, batch and token counts, adapter lineage, and all hashes. Assert parity in LoRA/quantization, batch/accumulation, prompt, selection (final epoch only), decoding, compiler/evaluator, and hidden tests. If a parity or lineage assertion fails, **STOP** that run without silently repairing its outcome.

## Frozen outcomes and decisions

For each seed and A task ID, join pre-B status to each branch's post-B status. Report the full `PASS→PASS`, `PASS→FAIL`, `FAIL→PASS`, `FAIL→FAIL` table, by seed, A subskill, archetype, and aggregate. Acquired-task **retention** is `PASS→PASS / pre-B PASS`; **forgetting** is `PASS→FAIL / pre-B PASS`. Compute branch differences on the **same pre-B PASS IDs**; the denominator is identical across the two branches of a seed. Co-primary H1 uses the unweighted mean of three seed retention differences, in percentage points, with the per-seed replay >=50% floor. Co-primary H2 uses replay minus naive unweighted three-seed mean post-B pass@1 across all 32 EVAL_A tasks. Report `FAIL→PASS` separately so aggregate improvement cannot be mistaken for preservation. Report raw numerators and denominators and all task-level paired transitions.

For B, report pre-B, post-naive, and post-replay pass@1 overall, for each subskill and for each of four archetypes, plus per-task transitions and per-seed gains from the same pre-B B scores. A **broad B-learning claim is eligible** only if the naive branch in **every seed** gains at least **12/32** B tasks overall, at least **4/16** net passes in **each** B subskill, and at least **2/8** net passes in **each** B evaluation archetype. Net gain means post-B pass count minus pre-B pass count in that slice. If this fails, B observations remain reportable but no claim about replay preserving broad B plasticity is allowed. If eligible, H3 passes only when replay's unweighted three-seed mean B net gain is >=75% of naive's mean net gain, replay has positive net gain in each B subskill in every seed, and no seed's replay overall B score is more than 9/32 below its naive score. These gates avoid Phase 3B's narrow B acquisition being called broad learning.

Measure frozen 64-item non-GOCO regression at base, post-A, post-naive-B, and post-replay-B with the prior exact evaluator/prompt regime (including its 32-token output cap), without changing the suite. H4 **severe collapse** occurs if any post-A, naive, or replay checkpoint scores more than **9 of 64** below base, or if a replay checkpoint scores more than 9 of 64 below its seed-matched naive checkpoint. Report all item-level paired changes; a small fixed suite is a sentinel, not proof of general model-capability preservation.

**PASS (retention-valid replay replication)** requires eligible A acquisition and H1, H2, H3 (including broad naive B eligibility), and H4. **FAIL** means B-stage results are complete but any conjunctive threshold fails. **STOP** means pre-B ineligibility, missing/corrupt data or provenance, schedule/compute mismatch, compiler/evaluator defect, or incomplete checkpoint/evaluation: record what happened and do not impute a PASS/FAIL. If naive B eligibility fails after B training, report the experiment as FAIL for the broad joint claim and explicitly withhold the plasticity claim. Retention-only findings may be described according to H1/H2 but do not convert the joint result to PASS. No decision is made from aggregate A alone.

## Statistical analysis fixed before outcomes

The observed unit is a frozen task ID; each appears in all three seeds and matched branches. The three seeds are prespecified, not sampled or replaceable. Per-seed rates are averaged **equally**, never pooled as if the 96 seed-task observations were independent. A task-cluster bootstrap draws 32 A task IDs with replacement, preserving all seed/branch observations and pre-B transitions for each draw; 10,000 draws using RNG seed **20261221**, percentile 2.5/97.5 descriptive intervals for the H1 retention difference and H2 aggregate pass-rate difference. Recompute retention denominators inside each draw; a zero-denominator draw is discarded and its count reported. H1 is conditioned on acquired task IDs and therefore estimates a different quantity from H2. Report per-seed and per-subskill effects, not only the mean.

For B, independently resample its 32 task IDs as clusters with all seed/condition observations attached; report descriptive intervals for naive and replay net B acquisition and replay-minus-naive gain. For non-GOCO, resample its 64 fixed item IDs with all conditions attached; report descriptive paired score differences and intervals separately from the fixed 9-item collapse rule. Since archetypes are four constructed blocks per capability and tasks inside an archetype can correlate, also report the four archetype-specific effects and an **archetype-block sensitivity** (resample four whole archetypes, preserving their IDs and repeated seeds); label its intervals unstable given only four blocks. The task-cluster intervals are conditional on this constructed suite, not population generalization. No bootstrap interval changes the deterministic PASS/FAIL/STOP gates; no post hoc multiplicity or subgroup gate changes.

## Changes from Phase 3B

1. Fresh Phase 3C A/B training and confirmatory evaluation items, task IDs, cases, and three seeds; no 3A/3B evaluation prompts or references used as Phase 3C evaluation items.
2. Pre-B A acquisition is an explicit every-seed hard STOP with overall, both-subskill, and each-archetype floors; Phase 3B had no acquisition gate.
3. Exact acquired-task pass→pass retention and complete-suite post-B A performance are conjunctive co-primary outcomes; Phase 3B's primary was aggregate A alone.
4. `FAIL→PASS` reacquisition and all paired transitions have explicit interpretation.
5. Broad naive B acquisition must appear in both B subskills and all four archetypes before a broad plasticity claim; Phase 3B required only positive naive mean B.
6. Replay B gain is compared to naive **gain from pre-B**, not only post-B score.
7. Lower similarity thresholds, normalized structure/AST proxy flags, per-item distance buckets, and explicit review are frozen; Phase 3B's 0.98 rejection alone did not characterize lower overlap as fully.
8. Uncertainty is specified separately for retention, aggregate A, B, and non-GOCO, including task clustering and archetype-block sensitivity.
9. Post-A non-GOCO severe collapse is part of the gate, and essential final adapters/raw records require verified off-machine restore, closing Phase 3B's local-only risk.
10. No ratio optimization: this is an exact 20% replication with a new retention-valid prerequisite.

## Unresolved scientific risks

- The A recipe may miss the strict pre-B threshold; the scientifically correct result is STOP before B, with no adaptive rescue.
- Hand-constructed tasks and two archetypes per subskill can overstate generalization despite prompt/reference separation; structural similarity below 0.98 remains visible in the audit.
- Five hidden cases per task may miss semantic errors, and compiler success does not establish full correctness outside those cases.
- Three fixed seeds, 32 A and 32 B evaluation IDs, and four archetypes give limited precision; bootstrap intervals do not turn this into a population sample.
- Conditional retention denominators select tasks the A adapter could solve; H1 is therefore not interchangeable with H2 or a general ability measure.
- 20% replay displaces B examples; a replay B deficit may reflect reduced B exposure, even with matched total compute.
- A single prompt/scorer/compiler and a 64-item regression sentinel limit external validity and cannot prove broad capability preservation.
- Immutable remote storage availability and adapter restore are execution-stage dependencies; design does not itself archive future adapters.
