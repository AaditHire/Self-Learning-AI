C12 Training schedule. The 60 ledger training slot IDs are sorted in ascending UTF-8 order. For paired training seed s and epoch e in {1, 2, 3}, the epoch order is that sorted list shuffled in place by `random.Random(s + 100003 * e).shuffle` (the DEV2 schedule rule). Both conditions use the same slot order, each with its own condition's example and the C9 model-facing message. Each cell has 180 exposures and 24 optimizer steps (microbatch 1, gradient accumulation 8, with the per-epoch remainder handled exactly as in `scripts/train_phase3c_dev2.py`); all other hyperparameters follow protocol §3. Cells run in seed order 20280117, 20280223, 20280329, 20280411, 20280507, ISOLATED before COMPOSITION within each seed. The evaluated checkpoint is the end of epoch 3.

C13 Evaluation order, RNG and scoring. The global evaluation seed is 20290307 (chosen prospectively; a repository search found no prior use). The task RNG seed is the first 8 bytes, read big-endian, of SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string `20290307|<paired training seed>|<task_id>`, reduced modulo 2^32. It is identical for both conditions and is applied to Python `random`, NumPy, Torch CPU and CUDA immediately before each generation. Own-training diagnostics use task_id = the training slot ID (C9) and run over slots in ascending slot-ID order. The confirmatory order within every cell is: primary, sanity and structural task IDs each sorted ascending, then interleaved in repeating groups (primary, primary, sanity, structural) until exhausted (64 positions); positions of secondary tasks dropped under C11 are skipped. Generation is greedy (`do_sample=False`, `num_beams=1`, 512 new tokens) with the user template `prompts/phase1t_user_template.txt`, the system prompt `prompts/phase1t_system.txt` and first-fence extraction. A task passes when the extracted program, run by the pinned compiler with a 3-second timeout, succeeds on all five cases with normalized output equal to `expected_stdout`. The descriptive exact-target indicator compares `normalized_code` (from `scripts/validate_phase2b_data.py`) of the extracted program and of the target.

C14 Analyzer. Let E[s, t] = pass(COMPOSITION) - pass(ISOLATED) for the five paired seeds (ascending) and the 32 primary tasks; the 8 blocks are the primary `block_id`s, 4 per domain, each with 4 tasks (sorted by task_id). The point estimate is the mean of E. The +20 pp criterion is evaluated exactly as an integer: the sum of E is at least 32 (= 0.20 x 160). The interval uses `numpy.random.default_rng(20290119)`. In each of 10,000 replicates, draw in this order: seed indices `rng.integers(0, 5, size=5)`; then numeric block indices `rng.integers(0, 4, size=4)` over the numeric blocks sorted by block_id; then array block indices likewise. The replicate statistic is the mean of E over the drawn seeds and all tasks of the drawn blocks. The interval is `numpy.quantile(draws, [0.025, 0.975], method="linear")`; the lower-bound criterion is strictly greater than 0. Labels: INDETERMINATE_INSUFFICIENT_ACQUISITION if any of the 10 cells has fewer than 54/60 own-training semantic passes; analysis refuses (STOP) unless all 10 cells have complete, valid primary records; otherwise CONFIRMATORY_SUPPORT_UNDER_CONF1 if both numerical criteria hold, with the suffix _WITH_HETEROGENEITY when any protocol §4 qualification fails (every seed difference >= 0; at least 4 of 5 seed differences > 0; both domain mean differences > 0; at least 6 of 8 block mean differences > 0), and NOT_CONFIRMED_UNDER_CONF1 otherwise. The sanity qualifier is noncatastrophic iff the mean sanity difference is at least -10 pp and no seed difference is below -25 pp, computed over the kept sanity tasks; SANITY_NOT_EVALUABLE if fewer than 12 were kept. Descriptive only: OR-containing (G1, G4) versus OR-free (G2, G3, G3P) blocks by domain; Structural Transfer rates and failure stages; own-training semantic and exact-target rates and their paired differences; paired discordance; K7 output classes (R2-C).
