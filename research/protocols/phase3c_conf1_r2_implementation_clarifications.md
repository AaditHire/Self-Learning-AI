# CONF1 R2 implementation clarifications (project head)

Status: project-head clarifications of the frozen R1 (LF-normalized SHA-256 `4e0ca5c576746ab51fce3414085fd698003a37f745dcb2004cdf8d8c14b3af31`), the frozen R2 (LF-normalized SHA-256 `2fea0bc3846e0ab7da2161a71e53f6073c2c7107c62316d784f61259eb1e3c8c`; freeze manifest SHA-256 `e498f31a66caeb19633bdf1a229716b8942dd180c16910dac1246c5d93693375`) and the retained paired-scaffold normalization. Recorded 2026-10-05, before any Attempt 004 construction or model use. No rule, threshold, population, estimand or gate changes. If an item conflicts with a frozen artifact, the frozen artifact wins and construction STOPs.

C7 Statement kinds and K-row liveness. Statement units are those of the accepted I1 splitter (`conf1_r1.interp.split_statements`). Every unit of every training program and primary canonical reference must classify as exactly one of the following; any other unit is FAIL.
- K1 DECODE. Numeric: `NUMBER n.` and `INPUT(n).`. Array: `IMPORT strings.`, `SENTENCE line.`, `INPUT(line).`, the `strings.SPLIT(line, "|")` declaration, the four `strings.TO_NUMBER(parts[k])` declarations and `NUMBER[] values=[a,b,c,d].`
- K2 TRAVERSAL. Forward: `LOOP (NUMBER i=<start> TILL <bound>, i++)` with the domain's frozen start and bound. Reverse (training only; direction is a parameter under R1 decision C): the counter declaration (`NUMBER i=n.` or `NUMBER i=3.`), `LOOP (i>=<lower>)` with the domain's frozen lower bound, and `i-=1.`.
- K3 PRED[p]. An IF whose condition is exactly predicate p's frozen ledger expression.
- K4 INDICATOR_SET. Indicator declarations `NUMBER hitX=0.`, per-item resets `hitX=0.` and IF-child sets `hitX=1.`.
- K5 ACCUMULATE. `NUMBER total=<k>.` with a nonnegative integer k, and `total+=(<term>).` where the term is T-SUM `hitP+hitQ` or T-JOINT `hitP*hitQ` in training, or a product of exactly two indicators in primary references (R1 R-CF1).
- K6 DISPLAY. `DISPLAYNL(total).`
A K row (K1-K6, and K3 separately per predicate) is live in a condition's same-domain training union when at least one statement of that kind, in at least one example of the union, has a single-statement-deletion mutant that is killed on that example's selected cases (clarification C3, compiler-verified) and is not excluded by R2-A. P4(ii) requires, for each condition and domain, liveness of K1, forward K2, K4, K5, K6, and K3 for every predicate used by a primary task of that domain. Liveness of T-SUM and T-JOINT is reported, not required across conditions (the declared treatment asymmetry).

C8 P3 implementation. The paired-scaffold comparison is implemented as exact text equality, within each matched slot, of (a) the two target sources after replacing only the marked treatment expression (`hitP+hitQ` or `hitP*hitQ` inside `total+=(...)`), (b) the two complete model-facing chat serializations (system prompt and user message) after replacing only the marked treatment clause, and (c) the slot fields and the ordered case inputs and case IDs. This is stricter than the normalization the frozen rule permits; any difference fails. Token parity (supervised difference at most 2%, full difference at most 5% relative to ISOLATED, no example above 320 tokens) is computed on the exact chat serialization used by the training runner (`scripts/train_phase2a_qlora.py` TokenDataset), using only the pinned tokenizer files.

C9 Condition-neutral model-facing identifiers. The training user message is `Task <slot_id>` followed by a blank line and the prompt, where `<slot_id>` is the condition-neutral ledger slot ID (for example `CONF1-TR-NU-01-V0`), identical for both members of a pair. Condition-specific example IDs are record keys only and never appear in model-facing text. Reason: the frozen paired-scaffold rule requires the model-facing wrapper to be identical across conditions outside the marked treatment clause; the historical builder's condition-labelled example IDs (`CONF1-ISOLATED-TR-...`, `CONF1-COMPOSITION-TR-...`) would place the condition name in the wrapper.
