## Frozen rules and the internal representation

Authority: Coverage-v3 V3.2's total typed source mapping and V3.3's narrow
equivalences, with the delegated interface's complete nested equivalence list.
No new frozen schema or catalog ID is introduced. Descriptive catalog labels
remain implementation labels for frozen rule `V3.3`.

- AND/product: two exact frozen Boolean predicates versus multiplication of
  two source-proved 0/1 indicators, **local pair-joint relation only**.
- OR/sum: two Boolean predicates versus the typed truth indicator of
  `add(a,b)>0`, with actual source-derived indicator truth identity.

`EquivalenceRegionCertificate` binds the frozen catalog metadata, source and
contract members, internal edges, all physical incoming/outgoing edge ports,
source locations, expression operand/result types, target role, parsed
loop/control paths, alpha mapping, complete local proof and its actual
indicator write/range/reaching evidence. Exact statements are discovered from
parsed sources, not from user flags or labels.

The implemented closed contribution interface is deliberately narrow:
`IF(condition){displayed_accumulator+=1}` and, for the pair-joint alternative,
`displayed_accumulator+=(indicatorP*indicatorQ)`. The exact singleton unit
update is an explicit IF-to-truth-indicator realization in the V3.2 IF/indicator
choice grammar. Its false path leaves the accumulator value unchanged; its
true path adds exactly one. The source-proved product adds exactly the same
integer-01 contribution for the four local operand truth assignments. OR's
sum comparison is checked against its exact two-input positive-sum rule.
This bridge is not an atomic Boolean/integer cast or a new general algebraic
rewrite. Any other child statement, update target, increment, operand, nested
control context or unrelated arithmetic fails rather than being swallowed.

Regions include the complete selected contribution statement, all its original
expression/operator nodes and all semantic primitive occurrences. Common
indicator declarations, resets and predicate-controlled definitions outside
these regions are ordinary typed correspondences; they are not ignored or
marked irrelevant. Their actual range/current-iteration evidence is consumed
by the local proof. Differing unmatched indicator scaffolds are not yet
supported and cannot receive a successful certificate.

All graph items are retained, including unused physical nodes and both sides'
differing predicate multiplicity. Each belongs exactly once to ordinary typed
correspondence or a region. Incident edges are region-owned; a cross-region
edge is owned by its destination region. Both boundary lists may reference
that shared edge, but only one ownership claim is permitted. There is no
third/reference-only category in this mapper.

The eight requested partition fields are serialized under `partition`:
`source_ordinary_covered`, `source_region_covered`, `source_uncovered`,
`source_duplicate_coverage`, and their four `contract_` counterparts.
Acceptance requires both uncovered and duplicate sets to be empty. Ordinary
edges preserve exact endpoints, types, roles and ports. The entire outside
statement/binding/control/state/output scaffold must match after replacing
only certified contribution statements. Original state edges remain recorded;
conditional no-write versus unconditional addition of zero is justified only
by the exact region's closed value transition, never erased or silently made
one-to-one. No normal output is consulted to construct or accept a mapping.

The regional correspondence says these two closed contribution interfaces
agree within an otherwise identical program. It does **not** say distinct full
typed graphs are equal. Raw atomic AND/MUL keys remain distinct; no canonical
key or activity population is relabeled by this internal certificate.
